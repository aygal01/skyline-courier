Skyline Courier is an original 3D chase-view flight game: take off from Runway 36, thread golden rings over a river valley, mind your fuel and the crosswind, then bring her home for a precision landing. Three sorties — Ring Run, Canyon Dash and Dusk Delivery — with a full cockpit: lit airspeed, altitude and attitude gauges, a heading compass, fuel and throttle. Fly with the mouse or arrow keys, Shift / Ctrl or the wheel for throttle; stall warnings, fuel bonuses per ring and a synthesized WebAudio engine hum, wind and ring chimes. No build step and no network: just static files under game/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
